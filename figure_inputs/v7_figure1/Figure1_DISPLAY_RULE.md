# Figure 1 display rule — v7 formal

Author-approved design: three columns (necessity → reciprocal replacement route → discoveries), 180 × 130 mm, native layered SVG; 7/5 pt Arial, 8 pt panel letters. This rule is written before selecting/counting the new identity-only recurrence examples. It changes display only, not any scientific result.

## Instruction precedence

The formal work order explicitly requests one real aryl bromide connected to three real amines in panel a, replacing the invented R/zigzag icons. That later, specific instruction overrides the older sentence “the role-switch inset is the only concrete molecule.” Concrete structures therefore appear in the small recurrence illustration and the small role-switch inset. The figure still opens with the field-level need and a generic coupling, and the central route remains the visual centre; it does not open with a particular reaction or promote the recurrence example into evidence.

## Identity-only recurrence selection, prespecified

1. Use only analysis-unit A1 identity columns `screen`, `sub_1_smiles`, `sub_2_smiles` from the released analysis records. Use the two C–N screens `Buchwald-Hartwig|1` and `Buchwald-Hartwig|2`; the recurrence illustration concerns the molecule's recorded coupling partners, not one condition library. Deduplicate substrate pairs across these two screens. Do not use yield, product-formation, fitted share, correlation, prediction or any other outcome field; do not filter pairs by outcome or quantification status.
2. Canonicalize identities with RDKit only for identity matching and the following fixed simplicity tests; RDKit is not the artwork renderer. A candidate is neutral and single-fragment. Amines have at most ten heavy atoms and at most one ring, a neutral trivalent N bearing one or two hydrogens, and no halogen; exclude an N directly attached to a carbonyl/thiocarbonyl or analogous S/P centre (amide-type N is not an amine). A candidate aryl bromide has exactly one bromine bonded directly to an aromatic atom, at least one six-member aromatic ring, at most two rings and eighteen heavy atoms, and no other halogen. The initial attempt restricted the illustration to the ligand library and bromides with ten heavy atoms/one ring; it produced no example with three eligible small amine partners (maximum two). Its written twelve/fourteen-heavy-atom fallback was also empty. Preserve those empty-rule results and rules in formal QA. The present expanded C–N scope and eighteen-heavy-atom limit are fixed before executing their ranking, retain the same identity-only ranking, and permit a small two-ring aryl bromide to be displayed. No yields or outcomes informed any amendment.
3. Retain an eligible aryl bromide only if its recorded pair identities include at least three eligible different amines. Rank these bromides by decreasing number of distinct eligible amine partners, then increasing heavy-atom count, then canonical SMILES in ordinal order. Use the first.
4. Among that bromide’s eligible recorded amine partners, rank by decreasing number of distinct recorded aryl-halide partners across those same two screens (identity count only), then increasing heavy-atom count, then canonical SMILES in ordinal order. Use the first three. This favours common molecules within a fixed simple-molecule boundary, with deterministic ties.
5. Persist the complete eligible rank table and selected identity pairs in `selection.json`. Counts are selection evidence, not new reported scientific results; do not print them on the figure. Generate missing artwork with the installed ChemDraw 22 engine at ACS 1996 settings (14.4 pt bond length, 0.6 pt stroke, Arial 10 pt master), and use genuine existing ChemDraw vectors for identical molecules where available. Preserve native master geometry; uniform print scaling only.

## Scope and reproducibility

No new scientific analysis, result, denominator, threshold, pooled statistic or outcome-driven selection is authorized. The builder reads frozen display bindings and genuine chemistry vectors; it supports `--out`/`--output-dir`, `--data-root` and `--journal`. Final citation numbers come from the unified manuscript mapping frozen into `records.json`, not a draft/QA input. Current display outputs are reproducible in the source tree or a materialized fresh public-package tree. Existing legacy Figure 1 scientific tables/payload are preserved and identified as legacy; the v7 map has an explicit new display binding/readback.
