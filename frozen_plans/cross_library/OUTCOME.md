# Outcome: the owner's record wins outside the arrays, in eleven of thirteen reaction systems

Scored 1 October 2026 against `PREREGISTRATION.md`, after the hashes in `PREREGISTRATION.sha256` were
written. No amendment was made. Results: `06_RESULTS/cross_library/`.

30,556 sealed rows, 535 substrate pairs, thirteen arrays, none of them part of any analysis in this work.

## Primary endpoint — confirmed in its general form

D > 0 and both lower bounds are above zero, which the pre-registration names as the outcome that
licenses the unrestricted sentence.

| | value |
|---|---|
| Targets | 535 in thirteen arrays |
| Owner-only prediction, pooled R² | 0.127 |

| 95% interval, owning building blocks resampled within arrays | 0.057 to 0.119 |
| 95% interval, whole arrays resampled | 0.063 to 0.111 |

The pre-registered emulation predicted a median D of 0.161 on analysis pairs at these counts; the
measured value on the sealed pairs is 0.089. The emulation trains on a smaller array than the real
test and takes complete panels, while these pairs cover a median of 26 to 71 conditions of their
library, so a smaller measured value was expected.
